Thank you for helping us get started

Customers don’t leave because the product was bad.

They leave because nobody checked on them. Small business owners work hard to win a customer, then lose them quietly: no thank-you, no follow-up, a receipt scribbled on paper, a complaint lost in a busy WhatsApp chat.

Folotrak gives every owner the office they could never afford, inside the phone they already use.

Plans the day

The moment the app opens, the owner sees exactly what needs doing today.

Records every sale

In under 30 seconds, linked to the customer and the products sold.

Sends proper receipts

With the business’s own logo, shared on WhatsApp in one tap.

Remembers to follow up

Tells the owner who to check on, with the message already written.

Handles complaints

Every complaint gets a ticket and a caring reply until it’s solved.

Watches the stock

Warns the owner before a best-seller runs out.

Questions

Is this an investment?

No. Founding support is a gift to help us launch. You won’t own part of Folotrak or receive repayment. You’ll be recognised as one of the people who made it possible, and you’ll see exactly how your support was used.

Who is Folotrak for?

Small business owners who sell to repeat customers and run their business from a phone and WhatsApp: beauty and skincare, food and catering, fashion, salons, pharmacies and retail shops.

Does the app exist yet?

Yes. A working web pilot is being tested by business owners in Nigeria right now. Your support funds the proper Android and iPhone app and its launch.

How do I pay, and is it secure?

You can pay by card from any country through Flutterwave, a licensed payment company used by businesses across Africa. Supporters in the US can also give by bank transfer. See “Ways to give” above. We never ask for card details by message.

Can I give a different amount, or stay anonymous?

Of course. Choose “Give $250 or more” to enter any amount, and tell us if you’d rather not have your name shown on the wall.

Who is behind Folotrak?

Folotrak is built by Precious Omole, a customer experience and sales professional who has trained more than 120 people in customer service and wrote “6 Power Moves: 6 Secrets That Make People Buy Again & Again”.

I’ve spent my career helping businesses look after their customers. Again and again, I see good businesses lose good customers simply because they’re too busy to remember them. Folotrak is my answer: a tool that remembers for them, so they can grow.
