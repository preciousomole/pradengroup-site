How it works

Four steps from role to hire.

  1. Tell us the role

    Responsibilities, hours, time zone and the tools your team uses.

  2. We shortlist

    We pick trained candidates whose skills and experience match.

  3. We pre-interview

    We interview each shortlisted candidate before you do, and only pass on those who fit.

  4. You interview and hire

    You meet the candidates, make the decision, and we stay in touch to support the fit.

How we assess every candidate

Five things we check before anyone reaches you.

Communication

Clear spoken and written English, careful listening and the right tone for your customers.

Role skill

Tested through real scenarios: an upset customer, a cold call, an intake call, a messy inbox.

Reliability

Availability, responsiveness, punctuality and follow-through on what they commit to.

Tools

The software your role runs on, and how quickly they pick up a new system.

Remote readiness

Stable internet, a working computer, power backup, a quiet workspace and real overlap with your time zone.

Training follows the gaps. We don’t put everyone through the same course: each candidate gets targeted coaching on what their assessment shows they need.

FAQ

Hiring through Praden Hub.

How do I hire a remote virtual assistant or customer service rep from Nigeria through Praden Hub?

Email info@pradengroup.co with the role, working hours, time zone and tools. We shortlist trained candidates who match, pre-interview them, and send you only the people who fit. You run your own interview and make the final decision.

What roles can Praden Hub fill?

Customer service representatives, virtual assistants, administrative assistants, intake specialists (including legal intake) and sales development representatives (SDRs), all for remote work.

Which countries do you work with?

We work with companies based in the United States and the United Kingdom.

Are your candidates trained for US and UK customers?

Yes. Our training is built around live role-play, real script practice, written communication and remote work habits, so candidates are ready to serve customers and teams in the US and UK.

Do you interview candidates before sending them?

Yes. Every candidate is pre-interviewed by us before you meet them, so your time goes only to people who already match the role.

What happens if a candidate is not a good fit?

We do not recommend people who do not match your role. If no one in our pool fits, we tell you rather than send someone unsuitable.

How are candidates engaged?

Our candidates work remotely from Nigeria. Most employers engage them as independent contractors or through an employer of record (EOR). We confirm the arrangement with you before any interviews.

How much does it cost?

Email us with the role and we will explain how we work and any fees before you commit to anything.
