About the founder

Precious “Praden” Omole

Founder & Lead Consultant, Praden Hub

Precious “Praden” Omole is the Founder and Lead Consultant of Praden Hub, a customer experience consulting, solutions, and training firm. With over 8 years in customer experience and more than 3 years in training and facilitation, Precious has worked across customer support, customer success, sales development, and business growth, helping founders and growing businesses turn customer experience into a revenue driver, not just a support function.

He is the author of Six Secrets That Make People Buy Again & Again, a book on customer loyalty, retention, and relationship-driven growth. Alongside Praden Hub, Precious is Co-Founder of Folotrak, a mobile app that helps business owners follow up with customers, keep track of their buyers and sales, and see what sells best, so no customer is forgotten. It puts the same customer experience thinking Praden Hub teaches into a tool owners can use every day.
