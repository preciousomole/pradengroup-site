About

Precious "Praden" Omole

Founder & Lead Consultant, Praden Group

Precious "Praden" Omole is the Founder and Lead Consultant of Praden Group, a Lagos-based Customer Experience consulting and training firm. With over 8 years in customer experience and more than 3 years in training and facilitation, Precious has worked across customer support, customer success, sales development, and business growth, helping founders and growing businesses turn customer experience into a revenue driver, not just a support function.

He is the author of Six Secrets That Make People Buy Again & Again, a book on customer loyalty, retention, and relationship-driven growth. Alongside Praden Group, Precious is also Co-Founder of an HR technology venture building an AI-powered recruitment platform connecting employers with job-ready talent, work that feeds directly back into how Praden Group trains professionals to be genuinely hire-ready.
