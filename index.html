About the founder

Precious “Praden” Omole

Founder & Lead Consultant, Praden Hub

Precious “Praden” Omole is the Founder and Lead Consultant of Praden Hub, a customer experience consulting, solutions, and training firm. With over 8 years in customer experience and more than 3 years in training and facilitation, Precious has worked across customer support, customer success, sales development, and business growth, helping founders and growing businesses turn customer experience into a revenue driver, not just a support function.

He is the author of Six Secrets That Make People Buy Again & Again, a book on customer loyalty, retention, and relationship-driven growth. Alongside Praden Hub, Precious is Co-Founder of Folotrak, a mobile app that helps business owners follow up with customers, keep track of their buyers and sales, and see what sells best, so no customer is forgotten. It puts the same customer experience thinking Praden Hub teaches into a tool owners can use every day.

What does Praden Hub do?
We’re a customer experience consulting, solutions and training firm. We help founders and growing businesses turn customer experience into growth through consulting (CX strategy and startup advisory), solutions (customer care and follow-up systems, and our app, Folotrak) and training for support, CX and sales teams and job seekers.
Who is your training for?
Customer support and CX teams, sales and business development teams, and job seekers preparing for customer-facing roles in support, CX and sales. Business owners who want their team to treat customers better are welcome too.
How does the online training work?
Classes are live and online on weekends, so you don’t have to take time off work. Groups are small (up to 40 people) and built around role-play and real script practice, so you leave able to do the work, not just talk about it.
When does the next cohort start, and how much is it?
Dates and fees vary by programme. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll send you the next start date, the fee and how to register.
Can you train my company’s team?
Yes. We run corporate training for customer support, CX and sales teams, online or as in-person workshops. Get in touch and tell us about your team and what you want to improve.
What happens in a consulting or advisory session?
We look at how customers experience your business, from first message to repeat purchase, and help you decide what to fix first. Startup advisory sessions focus on retention, customer service standards and growth without spending more on ads.
Do participants get a certificate?
Yes. Each Praden Hub certificate carries a code that anyone can check on our certificate verification page.
How can an employer check that a certificate is genuine?
Go to Verify a certificate and enter the code printed on it. You’ll see straight away whether it was issued by Praden Hub.
What is Folotrak, and when can I use it?
Folotrak is our mobile app that helps business owners follow up with customers, keep track of buyers and sales, and see what sells best. It’s in pilot with business owners and coming to Android and iPhone. Ask for early access on WhatsApp.
How do I get the ebook?
6 Secrets That Make People Buy Again & Again costs ₦5,000. Buy it here and you’ll receive it after payment.
